About EECS Club

Fostering innovation and technical excellence at IISER Bhopal since 2024.

💡

Innovation

We encourage creative thinking and problem-solving through hands-on projects, novel architectures, and collaborative workshops.

💻

Technical Rigor

Develop deep expertise in programming, electronics, machine learning, robotics, and cutting-edge engineering toolchains.

👥

Open Community

Connect with like-minded peers, mentors, and faculty across physics, chemistry, biology, math, and computer science in an inclusive learning environment.

🛠️

Hands-on Culture

We believe engineering begins where theory meets action. We provide workbenches, microcontrollers, and sensors to build, test, and ship real systems.

DIRECTIVE // MISSION

Our Mission

To create a vibrant community of tech enthusiasts who collaborate, learn, and build innovative solutions to real-world problems.

[>] WHAT WE DO:

  • Organize workshops, bootcamps, and technical training sessions
  • Host hackathons, project exhibitions, and coding competitions
  • Develop collaborative open-source and hardware research projects
  • Connect with industry experts, alumni, and faculty researchers
  • Provide hardware inventory loans, compute guidance, and peer mentorship

WHY JOIN EECS?

Gain hands-on hardware lab access, join active national hackathon squads, receive direct mentorship from senior researchers and faculty, and transform theory into tangible engineering achievements.

WHY JOIN & WHO CAN APPLY

We welcome all IISER Bhopal students who are passionate about technology, regardless of their department or prior programming experience.

Key Member Benefits:

  • Access to hands-on hardware lab benches, microcontrollers, and compute
  • Opportunity to work on real-world engineering and research projects
  • Mentorship from senior students and EECS faculty advisors
  • Team formation & funding support for national hackathons
  • Networking with industry professionals and research alumni
  • Certificate of active participation for club contributors

Open Domains & Roles:

[⚡ AI & Deep Learning] [🛠️ Embedded & Robotics] [🌐 Web & Systems] [🎪 Event Logistics & Ops] [🎨 Media & Creative Design]
HOW TO JOIN & ELIGIBILITY

How to Join:

Recruitment happens through our annual Trainee Induction cycle. When applications open, submit your technical interest form, complete a hands-on beginner assignment, and attend a friendly interview discussion with club coordinators.

Eligible Academic Streams:

[✓] Engineering Sciences (EECS, DSE, ChemE)
[✓] BS-MS Natural Sciences (Physics, Chemistry, Math, Bio, Earth)
[✓] Economic Sciences & All Academic Years (1st to 5th Year)
